# Code of Conduct

Folio welcomes contributors and users regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Participation in the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces means following these expectations.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and ask for clarification when needed.
- Respect different levels of experience and accept corrections without personal attacks.
- Keep discussions relevant and protect other people's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** tools to flag abusive issues, comments, or accounts. If you need a private conversation with the maintainer, use a contact method they have chosen to publish on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/aravmdn). If none is available, request a private contact channel in an issue without posting personal or sensitive details.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, warn participants, or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should consider severity, context, and repeated behavior. Do not retaliate against someone for reporting a concern in good faith.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
